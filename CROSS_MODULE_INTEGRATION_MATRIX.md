# LedgerAI PH — Cross-Module Integration Matrix

| Source Module | Target Module(s) | Integration Mechanism & Trigger | Data & Accounting Impact Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2. Sales & Receivables** | 6. Accounting & GL, 9. Tax & BIR, 12. Reports, 13. Approvals | `postSalesInvoice` / `postCustomerPayment` / `postCreditMemo` | Creates `JV-SI-*` / `JV-CR-*` / `JV-CM-*`; records Output VAT & CWT rows in `tax_calculations`; updates SI `balanceDue`; reflects immediately in Trial Balance & AR aging. |
| **3. Procurement & Payables** | 4. Inventory, 6. Accounting & GL, 9. Tax & BIR, 13. Approvals | PO → GRN → 3-Way Match → `postPurchaseBill` / `postSupplierPayment` | Creates `JV-PB-*` / `JV-SP-*`; records Input VAT & EWT (BIR Form 2307 data source); reduces PB `balanceDue`. |
| **4. Inventory & Warehouse** | 3. Procurement, 6. Accounting & GL | Stock receipt & stock adjustments (`operations.ts`) | Updates `inventory_items.quantityOnHand`; posts inventory & COGS movements cleanly inside `OPEN` periods. |
| **5. Cash & Banking** | 6. Accounting & GL, 13. Approvals, 15. Audit | `postCashTransaction`, `postBankDeposit`, `postCashCount`, `BankReconciliationService.approveMatch` | Posts cash journals; scores & matches `bank_transactions` against `POSTED` GL entries; syncs `bank_statements.status = 'RECONCILED'`; computes live book balances per `bankAccountId`. |
| **6. Accounting & GL** | All Financial Modules & 12. Reports | `validateTransactionDateAndPeriod`, `resolveCompanyControlAccount`, `RecurringJournalService` | Enforces `lockDate` and period status (`OPEN`/`SOFT_CLOSED`/`CLOSED`) across all write routes; drives Trial Balance, GL, Income Statement, Balance Sheet. |
| **7. Fixed Assets** | 6. Accounting & GL | Asset capitalization & depreciation schedules (`operations.ts`) | Requires linked GL journal entry before marking `POSTED` (blocked unbacked `POST` via `journey.ts`). |
| **8. Payroll & HR** | 6. Accounting & GL, 9. Tax & BIR | `POST /api/operations/payroll/process` | Atomic `db.transaction` creates `payroll_runs` (`POSTED`), `payroll_items`, and balanced payroll GL journal (Salaries Expense Dr / SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, WHT, Cash/Payable Cr). |
| **11. Documents & OCR** | 6. Accounting & GL, 15. Audit | `POST /api/documents/:id/post-accounting` | Uses `resolveCompanyControlAccount` to pick active postable leaf COA accounts when creating accounting entries from extracted document payloads. |
| **13. Approvals & 14. Activity Center** | All Modules | `/api/journey/approvals/*`, `/api/journey/action/:entityType/:entityId`, `syncActiveWorkflowsOnDirectDecision` | Bidirectional status/step sync across `approval_workflows`, `approval_steps`, entity tables, and `audit_logs`. |
| **15. Audit & Governance** | 6. Accounting & GL | `POST /api/audit-advanced/adjustments/:id/approve` | Validates `auditEngagements.companyId`; posts balanced AJE to `journal_entries` (or classifies summary schedules as `'PASSED'`). |
| **16. Master Data & Admin** | All Modules | Dual-file `.lkey` / `.lai` offline licensing, per-company SQLite isolation (`CompanyManager`), RBAC (`RbacService`) | Isolates tenants into encrypted `.lai` / `.db_active.sqlite` workspaces; enforces SoD and module permissions across all API routes. |
